# Information, Not Flexibility

Research by Hongfei Guo, J. Miguel Marín and Helena Veiga.

This study separates realised information, neural functional form and forecast combination within Bayesian stochastic volatility models. It compares three equity markets and one-, five- and ten-day forecasts, alongside identification experiments and predictive-moment analysis.

[Replication archive, version 1.1.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22923099)

## Explore the code

- `code/bnsv/`: modelling, filtering, scoring and evaluation components.
- `code/scripts/`: forecast generation and reproduction entry points.
- `code/tests/`: synthetic tests that do not require restricted market data.
- `design/`: model and evaluation definitions.

See [README.md](README.md) for the complete run order. Reproduction of empirical results requires the documented lawful source data. Included evaluation records do not support arbitrary rescoring of a complete predictive distribution.
